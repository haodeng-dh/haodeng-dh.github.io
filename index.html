I am a control engineer with a PhD in Automatic Control from Paris-Saclay University (European label), defended on 16 October 2026. My doctoral work is on neural-network-based control: stability analysis of Echo State Networks and LMI-based controller design for them, including MIMO and adaptive ESNs. I have also worked on fault-tolerant control of descriptor systems and on observer design.

Before the PhD, I worked on state-of-health estimation of power modules at Fraunhofer ISIT, and on field-oriented and model predictive control of PMSM drives at Ecole Centrale de Nantes.

I am looking for industry positions in control engineering, starting January 2027. I am eligible for the EU Blue Card.

Research interests

  • Neural-network-based control with stability guarantees (Echo State Networks, reservoir computing)
  • LMI-based controller and observer synthesis
  • Fault-tolerant control under actuator and sensor faults
  • Robust control of LPV and polytopic systems
  • State estimation and sensor fusion
